# Accounts (Harbour Demo Bank, fictional)

## Savings account interest
The savings account pays a base interest rate of 0.05% per annum, credited monthly. Bonus interest of 1.5% per annum applies to the first SGD 50,000 when you credit your salary into the account each month.

## Current account
The current account is designed for daily spending and does not pay interest. Debit cards are linked to the current account.

## Daily transfer limits
The default daily limit for transfers to other banks is SGD 5,000. Limits can only be changed in the banking app.

## Statements
Monthly e-statements are available in the banking app for the past 7 years.
