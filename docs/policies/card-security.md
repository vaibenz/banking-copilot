# Card Security (Harbour Demo Bank, fictional)

## Freezing a lost or stolen card
If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately. A frozen card declines all new purchases, ATM withdrawals and online payments within seconds, while recurring bills already linked to the card continue to be paid. Freezing does not cancel the card, so you can unfreeze it in the banking app if you find it.

## Unfreezing a card
Cards can only be unfrozen by the customer in the banking app after logging in with two factor authentication. The support copilot cannot unfreeze cards.

## Card replacement
A replacement card is issued free of charge for cards reported stolen. Replacement for a lost or damaged card costs SGD 10. New cards are mailed to your registered address within 5 to 7 working days.

## Liability for unauthorised transactions
You are not liable for unauthorised transactions made after you freeze or report your card. For transactions before reporting, liability is capped at SGD 100 if you report within 24 hours of noticing.
