# Disputes (Harbour Demo Bank, fictional)

## Who can raise a dispute
You can dispute any debit or card transaction on your own accounts that you did not authorise, that was charged twice, or where goods or services were not received.

## Dispute window
Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date. Transactions older than 60 days cannot be disputed through the copilot and must be raised with a branch officer.

## What happens after you raise a dispute
The dispute is reviewed by the card investigations team. For disputes above SGD 50, a provisional credit is applied within 5 working days while the investigation is open. Most investigations are completed within 45 days, and you will be notified of the outcome by email.

## Freezing the card during a dispute
If you believe your card details have been stolen, freeze the card as well as raising the dispute, so further unauthorised charges are blocked.
