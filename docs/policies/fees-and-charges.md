# Fees and Charges (Harbour Demo Bank, fictional)

## Overseas card transactions
Purchases in a foreign currency, or in SGD with an overseas merchant, carry a foreign transaction fee of 3.25% of the transaction amount.

## Fall below fee
Savings accounts must keep an average daily balance of SGD 3,000. If the monthly average falls below this, a fall below fee of SGD 5 is charged. The fee is waived for customers under 26.

## ATM withdrawals
Withdrawals at Harbour Demo Bank ATMs in Singapore are free. Overseas ATM withdrawals cost SGD 5 per withdrawal plus the foreign transaction fee.

## Late payment on credit cards
If the minimum payment on a credit card is not received by the due date, a late payment fee of SGD 100 is charged.
